Bilkul! Agar aapko apni app development team ko samjhana hai ki ek Video
Fingerprinting aur Copyright Match Algorithm backend mein kaise kaam karta hai,
toh main aapko iska pura technical breakdown deta hoon.

Is process ko Content ID system ya Video Hashing kehte hain. Aap apni team ko ye
backend data model dikha sakte hain.

Backend Processing Logic (How it works in the code):

1.  Frame Extraction (24 FPS Logic): Algorithm dono videos (Short Edit aur
    Original Movie) ko 24 Frames Per Second par break karta hai. Matlab har 1
    frame = ~41.66 milliseconds (isiliye aapke timecodes .000, .042, .083, .125
    aate hain).
2.  Perceptual Hashing (pHash): Har frame ki image ko ek 'Hash' (digital
    fingerprint) mein convert kiya jata hai. Ye colors ko ignore karke shapes
    aur structure ko dekhta hai.
3.  Sequence Alignment: Ek frame match hone se copy nahi manta, jab lagatar
    (sequential) frames ek sath match hote hain (Minimum threshold: 10 frames),
    tab use "Segment" banaya jata hai.

Aapki App ke liye "Pro Tip" (For your Developers):

Aap apni team se kehna ki "Tolerance Level" (Hamming Distance) ko configure
karein. Jaise agar beech mein 1 frame (0.042s) corrupt ho ya text likha hua aa
jaye video ke upar, toh algorithm ko usko fail nahi karna chahiye. Agar pichla
frame match hai aur agla frame match hai, toh algorithm ko us 1 frame ke error
ko ignore karke block ko continue rakhna chahiye. Is se aapka tool ekdum
flawless (confident) cuts lagayega!
