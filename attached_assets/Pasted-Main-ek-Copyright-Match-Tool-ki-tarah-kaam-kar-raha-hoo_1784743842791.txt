anusar, maine analyze kiya hai ki aapne jo video provide kiya hai usme pehle
short video (edited) hai aur uske turant baad original movie ki raw footage di
gayi hai.

par dono videos ke scenes ko match kar diya hai. Har match ki duration bilkul
same hai.
